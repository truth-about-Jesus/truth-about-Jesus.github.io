After he had enjoyed the sumptuous meal prepared for him, as he thought, by his older son Esau,

在他享用完那頓他以為是由長子以掃為他準備的豐盛大餐之後，

\

Isaac spoke.

以撒說話了。

\

"Come near and kiss me, my son."

「過來吻我，我的兒子。」

\

Jacob approached cautiously and gave his father a kiss.

雅各小心翼翼地走近，給了他父親一個吻。

\

As soon as he caught the scent of Esau's clothing,

他一聞到以掃衣服的香味，

\

Isaac's heart welled up with love for his firstborn,

以撒心中充滿了對長子的愛，

\

and the fatherly blessing

和父親的祝福

\

poured out of him easily.

從他身上自然而然地傾瀉而出。

\

<center>💠</center>

\
\

"My son's scent is the fragrance of a field blessed by the Lord.

「我兒子的香氣是主賜福的田野的香氣。

\

God gives you the dew of Heaven, the wealth of the Earth, 

上帝賜予你天堂的露水，大地的財富，

\

an abundance of grain and wine.

豐富的穀物和葡萄酒。

\

Peoples serve you; nations kneel before you.

人民為你服務；國家在你面前跪下。

\

Be mighty over your brothers; 

對你的兄弟們要強大；

\

the sons of your mother 

你母親的兒子們

\

bow down to you.

向你鞠躬。

\

Those who curse you 

那些詛咒你的人

\

are cursed, 

被詛咒，

\

and those who bless you 

以及那些祝福你的人

\

are blessed."

受到祝福。」

\

<center>💠</center>

\
\

As her younger son

作為她的小兒子

\

received the blessing intended for the elder,

他獲得了原本應歸於長子的祝福，

\

Rebekah

麗貝卡

\

watched from the doorway,

從門口看著，

\

her heart rejoicing.

她心歡欣鼓舞。

\

Now,

現在，

\

God's prophecy 

上帝的預言

\

would be fulfilled!

會實現的！

\
