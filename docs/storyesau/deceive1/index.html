13 - Deception 1

欺騙1

"My father."

「我的父親。」


Jacob stood uncomfortably in the doorway to his father's room,

雅各不舒服地站在他父親房間的門口，


wearing his brother Esau's best clothes.

穿著他哥哥以掃最好的衣服。


His mother had covered his hands and neck with the hairy skins of the young goats he had brought her,

他的母已經親用他帶給她的小山羊的毛茸茸的皮遮住了他的手和脖子，


once she had prepared,

她做好準備後，


exactly the way Isaac loved,

正是以撒喜歡的方式，


the steaming, delicious dish of goat meat

那熱氣騰騰、美味的山羊肉菜


the younger twin now held in his hands.

現在，弟弟把它拿在手裡。


"I'm here," replied Isaac in his gentle way, perking up at the mouth-watering aroma.

「我在這裡，」以撒以溫和的方式回答，聞到令人垂涎的香味後精神一振。


Barely able to see anymore,

他幾乎看不見了，


he paused, uncertain.

他停頓了一下，不確定。


He was expecting Esau --

他正等著以掃回來——


eventually.

過一會兒。


"Who are you, my son?"

「你是誰，我的兒子？」


💠



"I am Esau, your firstborn," Jacob responded forthrightly,

「我是以掃，你的長子，」雅各直截了當地回答道，


as his mother had coached him.

和母親剛剛教他的方式一模一樣。


Isaac frowned a little.

以撒微微皺起了眉頭。


He'd thought he'd heard Jacob's voice...?

他以為他聽到了雅各的聲音......？


💠



Jacob continued, a little recklessly, as he knew his twin would:

雅各繼續說道，語氣有些魯莽，因為他知道他的雙胞胎兄弟會那樣說話：


"I have done what you commanded me, Father!

「我已經做到了你吩咐我的，父親！


Please sit up, and eat of my game,

請坐起來，吃我的鹿肉，


so your soul blesses me."

所以你的靈魂祝福我。」
