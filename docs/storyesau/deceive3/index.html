15 - Deception 3

欺騙3

As he nervously approached his father,

當他緊張地接近他的父親時，


Jacob willed himself to remain rock steady,

雅各強迫自己像磐石一樣堅定，


like his older brother.

像他的哥哥一樣。


Isaac reached out and touched

以撒伸出手來，感覺到了


Jacob's hands,

雅各布的手，


covered by the furry goat skins

被毛茸茸的山羊皮覆蓋


Rebekah had so thoughtfully provided.

麗貝卡如此周到地提供了。


The blind man breathed a sigh of relief... it was Esau...

盲人鬆了一口氣......這是以掃......


then hesitated,

然後猶豫不決，


his forehead wrinkling again.

他的額頭又皺了。


💠



"The voice

「聲音


is the voice of Jacob,

是雅各的聲音，


but the hands,

但是手，


the hands of Esau,"

以掃的手，」


he mused aloud,

他喃喃自語道，


as Jacob's heart fluttered.

當雅各的心怦怦直跳時。


He had not thought to imitate Esau's deeper voice...!

他竟然忘了模仿以掃低沉的聲音......！


But it was too late now.

但現在為時已晚。


💠



"You are he --

「你就是他——


my son, Esau?"

我的兒子，以掃？」


Isaac inquired

以撒詢問


one last time.

最後一次。


"I am,"

「我是他，」


Jacob replied, deepening his voice slightly.

雅各回答道，聲音稍微加深了。


💠



Isaac relaxed.

以撒放鬆了。


Grasping Jacob's hand

握住雅各的手


he smiled

他笑了


unseeing

看不到


at his beloved "older son."

他心愛的「大兒子」。


"Bring me your game, my son.

「把你的鹿肉帶給我，我的兒子。


I will eat,

我會吃的，


and my soul will bless you."

我的靈魂會祝福你。」
